BrainLite Support

BrainLite: Focus Training offers short exercises for attention and memory and keeps a record of your practice. If you get stuck or have a suggestion, email us.

Frequently asked questions

I can't sign in, or my practice history is missing.

Check your connection, then sign in using the same method and account you used before. If your history still looks wrong, email us with roughly when you last practiced and any error you see.

My latest session isn't in my history.

If your connection dropped during practice, reconnect and reopen BrainLite to give the app a chance to sync. If the session still doesn't appear, tell us which exercise you played and when you finished.

How do I manage an Apple subscription or ask for a refund?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions and select BrainLite. You can also manage subscriptions with Apple. For refund requests for App Store purchases, sign in at Apple's Report a Problem page.

I paid, but my membership isn't showing.

Check that the purchase appears in your Apple purchase history. Open BrainLite while connected and sign in to the BrainLite account you used when buying. If membership still doesn't appear, email us with the plan and purchase date.

How do I delete my BrainLite account?

Sign in, then open Me › Privacy & Account › Delete Account. Read the confirmation before continuing. Deleting your account removes your BrainLite practice records and cannot be undone. Manage any active Apple subscription separately in Apple's subscription settings. If you can't sign in, email us for help.
